Beta — Where to submit

Where should
this paper go?

Upload your paper or type its title and abstract. JournalCheck finds the journals and computer-science conferences that published the most similar work in the last five years, shows their fees and next deadlines, and drops any journal that fails its safety checks.

Your paper stays on your device. The PDF is read in your browser; only a few search terms are sent to OpenAlex.

How it ranks

Fit first,
then safety

Fit combines three signals from OpenAlex: how close in meaning a venue's recent papers are to your title and abstract (semantic search over the 50 closest papers), how many papers matching your most specific keywords it published in the last five years, and how much its published topics overlap your paper's subject areas. Journals that barely overlap are dropped, and a small share of the score comes from the h-index. Every journal then goes through JournalCheck's registry rules: listed high-risk journals are removed and sudden output jumps are flagged. Conference deadlines, ranks and acceptance rates come from the community-maintained ccfddl list, so they cover computer science only. A fit score is a starting point: read the venue's scope and recent issues before you submit.
